# FitSnack Privacy Policy

_Effective: October 16, 2026_

FitSnack ("the app", "we", "us") is published by Rotem Tal, Hermon 36, Mevaseret Tsion, Israel. This policy explains what data the app handles, where that data goes and what you can do about it.

The short version:

- FitSnack has no accounts. You never give us your name, email address or password.
- Your profile, schedule, workout history and streak are stored only on your phone.
- Your calendar is read on your phone to keep reminders out of your meetings. Nothing from it ever leaves your phone.
- With your permission, the app sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports so we can improve it. You can turn this off at any time in Settings.
- We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

## 1. Data stored only on your device

The app keeps the following on your phone, in its own private storage:

- **Your profile**: goals, intensity, work setting, workday hours and active days, sore spots and available equipment
- **Your schedule**: reminder times and the workouts behind them
- **Your activity**: completed workouts, streak and the days the app was opened
- **Your settings**: theme, quiet times, holiday country and calendar choices

We cannot see any of this. It is deleted when you use **Settings > Clear all data** or uninstall the app.

### Device backups

If you have device backup turned on (Google backup on Android, iCloud on iPhone), your operating system may include this data in the backup, so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Google or Apple under their terms and is not accessible to us. The anonymous analytics ID described below is not carried to a restored phone. A restored app starts with a new one.

## 2. Your calendar

If you allow calendar access, FitSnack reads when you are busy so reminders do not interrupt meetings, and it reads public holidays you choose to follow. This happens entirely on your phone. Event titles, attendees, locations and calendar names are never stored outside the app and never sent anywhere, including to our analytics.

You can deny or withdraw calendar access in your phone's settings at any time. The app keeps working without it.

## 3. Notifications

FitSnack schedules reminder notifications on your phone. Reminders are created by the app locally and are not sent through any server. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings.

## 4. Anonymous usage statistics (analytics)

To understand which features are used and where the app fails, FitSnack sends usage events to **Mixpanel**, an analytics provider, with servers in the United States.

### What is sent

- Which screens are opened and for how long
- Actions in the app, such as starting, finishing or skipping a workout, editing the schedule or changing a setting
- Whether reminders were delivered, opened or dismissed
- Summary values about your use, such as streak length, number of upcoming reminders, intensity and workout length
- Times of day in broad buckets only (morning, midday, afternoon, evening)
- Technical details: platform, app version and operating system version
- The country you picked for holidays, if you picked one
- A random ID created when the app is installed, so events from the same install can be grouped

### What is never sent

- Your name, email address, phone number or any account
- Anything from your calendar
- Text you typed, such as search queries or custom exercise names
- Exact times of your reminders or workouts
- Your sore spots or equipment, except as counts (for example "2 items")
- Advertising IDs or hardware identifiers
- Your location. The app tells Mixpanel not to derive a location from your connection

### When it is sent

- **In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland**, nothing is sent unless you agree. The app asks you once, with the switch off by default.
- **Everywhere else**, statistics are on by default.
- **Everyone** can switch them off at any time in **Settings > Analytics**. Switching off deletes any statistics not yet sent and replaces the random ID, so turning statistics back on later starts as a new, unconnected install.

Until the app knows which region you are in, it keeps statistics on your phone and sends nothing.

### How long it is kept

Mixpanel keeps events for one year, after which they are deleted.

## 5. Crash reports

If the app crashes, it sends a crash report to **Firebase Crashlytics**, a service provided by Google. A report contains the technical cause of the crash, the app version, the device model, the operating system version and an installation ID created by Firebase. It contains none of your profile, schedule, calendar or workout data.

Crash reports follow the same rules as the statistics in section 4. They are off until you agree in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, on by default elsewhere, and switching **Settings > Analytics** off stops them for everyone. Google keeps crash reports for 90 days.

## 6. Working out your region and work week

When you first set up the app, and until your analytics region is settled, it sends a request to **FreeIPAPI** (freeipapi.com). Like any website you visit, the service sees your phone's IP address and replies with the country it belongs to. The app uses that country for two things only:

- choosing which days are workdays by default
- deciding whether to ask for your agreement before sending statistics and crash reports

The country is not stored with your analytics events and the IP address is not stored by the app. If the request fails, the app falls back to the region set on your phone.

## 7. Why we process data (legal bases)

For users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland:

- **Analytics and crash reports**: your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it in Settings at any time.
- **Region lookup**: our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)) in setting sensible defaults and in asking for consent only where the law requires it.

Data stored only on your phone is processed by the app on your device and not by us.

## 8. Your rights and how to use them

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your data, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent.

Because FitSnack has no accounts, we have no way to tell which analytics data belongs to you unless you give us your install's analytics ID. To make a request:

1. Open **Settings** and copy your **Analytics ID**.
2. Email it to [rotem.tal37@gmail.com](mailto:rotem.tal37@gmail.com) with what you would like us to do.

We will answer within 30 days.

When you use **Settings > Clear all data**, or switch analytics off, the app deletes your analytics profile at Mixpanel and replaces the ID on your phone. After that, nothing connects the remaining past events to you or your phone, and they are deleted when the retention period above ends. If you want those past events deleted sooner, copy your Analytics ID and email us **before** clearing your data.

You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

## 9. Sharing

We share data only with the providers named above (Mixpanel, Google and FreeIPAPI), who process it for us. We do not sell or rent your data, share it for advertising, or combine it with data from other sources. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

## 10. International transfers

Mixpanel and Google store data in the United States. Where data from the EEA, the UK or Switzerland is transferred there, it is protected by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and its UK and Swiss extensions, or by Standard Contractual Clauses, as described in each provider's data processing terms.

## 11. Security

Data stored on your phone is protected by your phone's own app sandboxing. Data sent to our providers travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

## 12. Children

FitSnack is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used the app and statistics were sent, contact us with the Analytics ID and we will delete them.

## 13. Health information

FitSnack is a general wellness app and not a medical device. The sore spots you enter are used on your phone to pick suitable exercises, and are never sent anywhere except as a count.

## 14. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
